# Third-party data

## Tarotoo Tarot Card Meanings

The file `src/data/tarotoo-cards.json` is derived without textual modification
from the Tarotoo Tarot Card Meanings Dataset, commit
`8cee2b1abf399a90e760307ee4c447c4d0505cdd`.

- Source: https://github.com/Tarotoo-com/tarotoo-tarot-dataset
- Copyright: Tarotoo
- License: MIT
- Dataset citation: Tarotoo. *Tarotoo Tarot Card Meanings: Complete Dataset Repository and Infrastructure*, Version 2.0.0.

The dataset is used as symbolic grounding for entertainment and self-reflection,
not as medical, legal, financial, mental-health, or other professional advice.
